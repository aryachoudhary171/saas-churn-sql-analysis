# Business recommendations

These recommendations use only the generated query outputs. Values are from the synthetic dataset and should be treated as analysis practice, not real market benchmarks.

1. **Test an early retention intervention for Paid Search.** Its observed cancellation rate is 56.0%, compared with 29.6% for Referral. Test clearer onboarding and a day-30 check-in, then compare matched cohorts.
2. **Protect channel quality while reviewing spend.** Referral has the highest average paid lifetime value at $203.44 per customer in Q20. Evaluate LTV alongside actual campaign cost before reallocating budget.
3. **Improve first-year onboarding using cohort checkpoints.** Mean month-0 retention is 97.9% and mean observed month-12 retention is 63.3%. Track activation and support needs by cohort month before rolling out product changes.
4. **Review the $79 Business plan’s pricing presentation.** Business has the highest observed refund rate (1.1%; 51 of 4,703 payment rows). Test clearer feature and billing explanations before changing the list price, and compare refunds and conversion.
5. **Keep support load and customer experience visible.** The ticket volume is 0.882 tickets per customer on average (Q05). Track this alongside resolution-time percentiles and satisfaction completeness; use the results to find categories where faster resolution could reduce churn.
